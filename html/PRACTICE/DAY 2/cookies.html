CHOCLATE CHIP

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter,softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vannila extrtact
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups choclate chips

    • Instructions

  1. preheat your oven to 360°(175°) f
  2. In a large bowl,cream together the butter,white sugar,and brown sugar until smooth.
  3. Beat the eggs one at a time,then stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. combine flour,baking soda and salt &rarr Actually,combine the flour,baking soda,and salt in a separate bowl,then gradually stir into the wet mixture.
  5. Fold in the choclate chips(use your favourite brand)
  6. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto ungreased baking sheets (for extra crispiness.)
  7. Bake for 10-12 minutes or untill edges are golden brown (check every 2 minutes for perfection)
  8. Allow cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
